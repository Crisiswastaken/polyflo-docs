Polyflo stays out of the way. There is no account to create, no word cap, and no editor chrome. Open any app that accepts typed text, hold the hotkey, and release when you are done.

## Who it is for

Polyflo fits when you want a small, auditable tray app and pay-per-use speech-to-text instead of a subscription dictation suite.

- You dictate in **Indian languages** or mix them with English mid-sentence.
- You want **no monthly plan** and transparent Sarvam billing.
- You prefer an **open-source** desktop app you can build yourself.

If you need AI cleanup, mobile apps, or cross-device snippets, see [Polyflo vs Wispr Flow](/compare).

## What you need

- Windows 10/11 or macOS 10.15+
- A microphone
- A [Sarvam API key](https://dashboard.sarvam.ai/)
